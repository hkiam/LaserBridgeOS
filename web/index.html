The bridge can pause an unattended machine, but it depends on this appliance, the cable and the controller answering. It is not an emergency stop — use the machine's own.

        


        

          
ser2net is carrying the port
NOT WATCHED

          
ser2net moves bytes and has no opinion about them, which is exactly why it is kept as the permanent fallback (ADR 0011). It also means none of the following is happening while it is selected:

          
    
            
  • the machine's state, position and laser output are not being read;
    • 
            
  • a laser left on while nothing moves is not noticed, and not switched off;
    • 
            
  • a client that disappears mid-job gets no feed hold and no soft reset — the machine finishes whatever is in its planner buffer;
    • 
            
  • nothing is recorded about alarms, errors or the line that caused them.
    • 
          

          
Switch to laserbridged under Bridge service below to get them back. Either way, neither is an emergency stop — use the machine's own.
